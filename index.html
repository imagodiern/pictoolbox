Convert, compress and edit images online for free

PicToolbox is a free image converter, image compressor and photo editor in one page. No signup, no watermark and no upload: your pictures are processed on your own device.

Image converter: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP and ICO

Convert JPG to PNG, PNG to JPG, WebP to JPG, JPG to WebP or PNG to AVIF, and convert many images at once. Download them one by one or as a ZIP.

Image compressor: reduce photo size in KB or MB

Compress JPG, PNG and WebP images without a big loss in quality. Set a target size such as 200 KB for forms, emails and websites, and see exactly how much smaller each file got.

Resize and crop images

Resize photos by width, height or percentage, keep the aspect ratio, or crop to 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16 and other ratios for Instagram, YouTube thumbnails and profile pictures.

Online photo editor: filters, watermark, rotate and flip

Rotate, flip, add a text watermark, apply black and white or sepia, adjust brightness and contrast, pixelate, round corners, add a border or make a colour transparent.

Merge images and convert images to PDF

Combine photos side by side, stacked or in a grid, or turn JPG and PNG files into one multi-page PDF.

Favicon generator, colour palette extractor and image to Base64

Create favicon.ico and app icons from any picture, pull the main colours from an image, or convert an image to a Base64 data URL for HTML and CSS.

Frequently asked questions

Is PicToolbox really free?

Yes. There are no accounts, limits or watermarks added by the tool.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens inside your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

How do I compress an image to a specific size in KB?

Choose JPG, WebP or AVIF, enter a target size in KB, and click Process. The tool finds the highest quality that fits.

Which image formats can I convert between?

You can export PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, BMP and ICO. AVIF export depends on your browser.

Can I convert several images to one PDF?

Yes. Add your images, open the Images to PDF tab and create a PDF with one image per page.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, it works in any modern mobile or desktop browser.

PicToolbox is free and open source. Images are processed on your device and never uploaded.
